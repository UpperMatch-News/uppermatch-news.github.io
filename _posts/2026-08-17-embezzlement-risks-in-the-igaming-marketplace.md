## What Is Embezzlement and Why Does It Matter in iGaming?

Embezzlement refers to the fraudulent appropriation of assets by someone entrusted with their management. In the context of iGaming business listings and asset exchanges, embezzlement can occur at any stage—from falsified revenue reports in a website sale to the siphoning of funds during an online casino brokerage transaction.

Given the digital nature of iGaming assets, the risk is heightened:
- **Remote asset management** can make it easier for insiders to manipulate data.
- **Cross-border transactions** may complicate regulatory oversight.
- **High-volume, fast-moving funds** in online casinos and affiliate marketing assets can obscure discrepancies.

## How Embezzlement Impacts iGaming Asset Transactions

The consequences of embezzlement in the iGaming asset marketplace extend beyond financial loss:
- **Damaged reputation**: Both buyers and sellers can be affected if a transaction is later revealed to involve fraudulent activity.
- **Legal complications**: Cross-jurisdictional iGaming business acquisitions make it harder to recover stolen assets or prosecute offenders.
- **Asset devaluation**: Even a hint of impropriety can reduce the perceived value of iGaming website listings or affiliate marketing assets.
